## 2. The "stalls" were suspensions (measurement bug)

Each logged stall lines up to the second with the playback trace: 04:04:15 audio interruption began → 04:05:24 interruption ended = "65 s"; 04:05:32 began → 04:15:56 ended = "621 s"; 04:28:03 pause → 04:51:59 = "1,433 s" (started 04:28:06); 04:52:00 → 04:55:23 = "203 s". With nothing playing, iOS suspended PodSkipper; `MainThreadWatch`'s timer was suspended too, and on resume it counted the wall-clock gap since the main thread last answered. **None of the five is evidence of a frozen screen.** The severe unresponsiveness he saw on Library/Compare models is therefore **not established by this export** (and not disproved).

**Changed (`MainThreadWatch`):** time is counted only while the watch's own timer runs; off screen, a gap of more than 2.5 s between its ticks is a suspension and restarts the count (in front, iOS doesn't suspend, so a late tick counts in full). Each stall line says "app not in front" when it wasn't. **T:** `Pass56StallWatchTests`.

**Compare models measured on the simulator** with his 461 test results (`build/p56/bench-history.plist`, both model lists open; `testPass56CompareLoad`, Time Profiler `build/p56/cmp.trace`): swipes through the open lists took 1.5–4.9 s each, but the main-thread samples were dominated by XCTest's own accessibility snapshots (~16,000 samples) — the app's own layout was ~2,000 samples over 46 s. ## 4. Core AI Qwen3.5 2B "stuck at 72–73 %"

The bar is the work meter: Core AI reports nothing while it reads a prompt, so the read is counted from the clock at the model's measured speed, and the bar holds at the end of that estimate until the first answer token arrives. 72–80 % is where that estimate ends for a Basic/Hard sample (prompt read ≈ 100 s at Qwen3.5 2B's measured 10–12 tokens/s, decode-only port read one token per step). So "stuck at 72 %" means the prompt read ran far past its expected time, or never finished. Which (slow, hung, or killed) can't be told from this export.

**Changed:** when a part's prompt read passes 1.5 × expected + 20 s, the test panel / episode status says "Reading part N is taking longer than this model's usual speed: m:ss so far, about m:ss expected", and the closure note records the real reading time against the expected one (`CoreAIReadOverrun`). **T:** `Pass56ReadOverrunTests`. The bundle isn't on this Mac (`~/Developer/pk-coreai` has no Qwen3.5 2B), so the read was not reproduced in the Core AI lab.

## 6. Reader

**Why the trained candidates were rolled back.** On his phone, every on-device head training since 23 Oct was rolled back ("worse on the lab's ads or the lab's show after every round of learning"): it learns only the small head from 4 corrected and 1–2 agreement episodes, and the per-class lab check (policy 47) refuses any head that loses on either class. Pass 43/47 measured head-only learning at its limit; the body has to change, which is what the lab experiments below do. No new manual labels were needed for them.

**Where held-out readers cut the show** (Pass 53's show-held-out fold answers re-cut with today's detector, 71 labelled episodes, `build/p56/lab/showcut.py`, `overrun.py`): 1,319 of 1,651 s of show cut touches a labelled break (edges), 332 s stands alone. By the reader's own probability for those show sentences: ~400 s it scored under 10 % (the detector's edge walk, kept lead-ins and plug joins carried the cut there — e.g. a pharma pre-roll's cut ran 28 s into the interview, 2 Bears' Manscapes/Shopify read 22 s into the talk after it), ~430 s it scored over 90 % (several are guest tour-date plugs inside conversation and Peter Attia's disclaimer, which the labels call show — label policy, not only reader error). Category: the Hard sample's network promo is read as the show's own plug (S 0.97).

**Hard = 0** traced: the reader found the guest plug (lines 33–35) and the promo/credits (41–49) separately; `joinParts` joined them across four interview lines it read 51–78 % as show ("one run of plugs"), and `credits` renamed the whole block the outro → 14 show lines cut.

**Experiments** (show-held-out: each fold's reader never saw any episode of its shows; same three show groups as Pass 53; one reader per fold; `build/p56/<tag>/`, ~12–15 min a fold on MPS). heard = ad seconds heard per hour; show = show seconds cut per hour; 502 labelled breaks:
