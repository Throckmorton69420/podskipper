## 2. Detection as a system: the cut check (lab + unit)

**Harness.** `Pass45ReplayTests.testReplayCutCheckWithRetrainedReader` (unit test, skips without the export) re-runs `ModelCutCheck.verify` on every model attempt's proposed cuts in his export with the episode's current Reader segments, and again with Pass 44's held-out fold readers (`build/p45/heldout-reader.json`, made by `lab-segments` from `build/p44/full/probs`; no audio evidence), and writes `build/p45/replay.json` for `phone_eval.py --cuts`. Labels: the 58 Pass 44 episodes + #691 (this pass, Claude-labelled from its transcript: Tulsa King ×2, Netflix plug, theme, BlueChew, Shopify, BetterHelp, Acorns, closing song). On HEAD the replay reproduces the phone's saved #691 cuts and notes exactly.

**Reader precision where the cut check ignored it** (on the 13 labelled episodes the retrained Reader has already re-labelled on his phone): ads at 50–89 → 303 s of ads, 6 s of show; plugs ≥ 80 → 344 s of plugs, 18 s of show.

**Change** (`ModelCutCheck.readerStands`, break neighbours, `relabelNamedSponsors`):
- A Reader stretch stands where the model is silent when it is: an ad ≥ 90 (as before); an ad or network promo whose recording plays elsewhere and is spot-length (≤ 90 s, confidence ≥ 50 — a rerun's whole segment is longer: "Reaching Out"'s 216 s repeats are refused); an ad ≥ 70 or a plug/promo ≥ 80 whose own lines sell (offer, code, address, tickets, "new special", "out now"…); proven intros/outros (as before). A model's MOCK_AD / RECURRING_SEGMENT still vetoes.
- A break is read as one: a middling (≥ 50) Reader ad/plug touching (≤ 3 s) something already cut, whose lines sell, joins it (#691's Netflix plug after the spot). Never intros/outros, never vetoed stretches; each stretch at most once.
- A model "own plug" naming a sponsor inside a Reader ad ≥ 90 is that ad (#691's Shopify).
- Tried and rejected: trimming Reader-only plugs to their selling lines (+2–5 s/h heard for ≤ 0.5 s/h show saved).
