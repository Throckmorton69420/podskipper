### 1a. Library "could not be opened" (SQLite 4618)

After each of the three compiler aborts the relaunch failed to open the library 3–7 times in a few seconds: `NSSQLiteErrorDomain=4618` (SQLITE_IOERR_SHMOPEN, `default.store-shm`) with the app **active and protected data available**, ~9 GB free (cleanup log before/after). Each time it then opened untouched (Library Recovery shown; one launch took 58 s to the first screen). The recovery log (`library-recovery.json`) came out **empty** in the export although the background log recorded every failure — that file's write evidently failed at the same moment, so the free space and store-file sizes those failures saw were lost. Cause of the transient SHMOPEN: not established (no space shortage on record; what held or blocked the shared-memory file after an abort isn't visible to the app).

**Changed (`LibraryStore`):** an open that fails with a SQLite I/O (10 and extended, incl. 4618), busy (5) or locked (6) error is retried quietly — 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4 s (~10 s), off the main thread, on the plain opening screen — before Library Recovery shows. Damage (11), full disk (13), can't-open (14) and non-SQLite errors are not retried. A launch that got through after retries logs one line with what the first failure saw (store files, free space, SQLite meaning). Nothing is deleted, reset or replaced. **T:** `Pass55LibraryOpenTests`; the Pass 33 store tests (a folder where the store should be) still fail at once.

### 1d. Which screen stopped responding

New `MainThreadWatch`: a timer off the main thread asks the main thread to answer each second; after 3 s it writes down the screen showing (every titled screen names itself through `.watchName`), and when the main thread answers again the stall goes into the background log ("The screen stopped responding for N s (screen)"). A note left behind means iOS ended the app during the stall; the next launch says so. The next foreground watchdog will name its screen.

## 2. Speed and Audio: All Shows / This Show

Before: the scope picker only chose which settings the controls edit; what played stayed the show's own settings (if it had any). Now (his request):
- **Switching scope plays that scope's saved settings at once**, speed included (`PlayerEngine.hearDefault`: while "All Shows" is chosen the default plays, also for a show with its own settings). Switching writes nothing to either scope. The scope it opens on is not a switch.
- **Closing the sheet** plays what applies to the episode again (show's own, else default) with its speed, when a switch had played the other set; otherwise a speed chosen from the speed button stays.

- **Reset This Show…** (new, beside Use My Default for This Show): the show keeps settings of its own, set to how PodSkipper starts (1×, everything off, EQ flat — `SoundProfile.neutral`, equal to a reset default). Undo works as for the other resets. Disabled when the show's own settings already are that.

## 3. Sync: which service carries what

His export: "Sync through your Cloudflare storage: On" since 30 Oct 19:37; received 3 + 1 changed items from the other device (19:40, 19:52); every record sync after that was logged **"iCloud: received …"** although it went through Cloudflare (the relay replaces iCloud as the store; iCloud is not used for sync while it is on). And with the relay, changes from the other device were only fetched at launch, on returning to the app or on Sync Now — iCloud pushes, the relay can't.

**Changed:**
- Log lines name the service ("Cloudflare sync: received 19, sent 0 (your ad corrections 12↓ 0↑, …)" or "iCloud: …"), kind by kind.
- Settings: section "Sync" with **Syncing through: Your Cloudflare storage / iCloud**; "Last received through Cloudflare"; "Last sync through …"; iCloud's own device list always reads iCloud's store (it used to read whichever store was active) and says when iCloud isn't used.
- Diagnostics (This iPhone): **Sync goes through**, **Synced through it** (followed/unfollowed shows; positions, played and starred; listening and sound settings; each show's settings; bookmarks; stations; your ad corrections; ads found on another device), Other devices on your Cloudflare storage, last sent/received, last problem.
- While the app is open with the relay on, the relay is checked **every 60 s** (one listing plus changed files); the library is merged only when something changed.

**Which transport carries each kind (from the code):** with the relay on, everything above goes through R2 only (one sealed file per device, newest write per key wins; listening records merge per episode and part by time; corrections made on a device are kept over another device's); with it off, the same keys go through iCloud key-value storage. Deletions: unfollows are a synced record; corrections carry their own history. Offline: a failed send leaves `relayProblem` and is retried on the next push; what arrives later is merged with the library, whose own records win by time. **Not verified between his two devices** (needs both); unit tests cover the relay's exchange, conflicts, deletions, wrong setup, relaunch (`Pass54RelaySyncTests`).
