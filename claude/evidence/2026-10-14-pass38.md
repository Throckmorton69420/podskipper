## 1. Chart pinch moved, tilted and closed the sheet (area 1)

- **Reproduced (sim):** a pinch-in on the chart at 1× closed the whole Speed and Audio sheet (`testSoundChartZoom` probe, before the fix).
- **Root cause (sim, recognizer dump on the live hierarchy):** when the second finger lands, the recognizers above the chart include `_UITransformGestureRecognizer` **`com.apple.UIKit.ZoomInteractiveDismissPinch`**, `…ZoomInteractiveDismissSwipeDown` and `…LeadingEdgePan` on the presentation's hosting view — installed by `.navigationTransition(.zoom(sourceID: "audio", …))` — plus `_UISheetInteractionBackgroundDismissRecognizer` and the navigation swipes. `TwoFingerZoom.Coordinator.shouldRecognizeSimultaneouslyWith` returned `true` for all of them, so the zoom transition's scale-and-rotate pinch ran with the chart's (the tilt in his second screenshot). Also found: the chart's recognizer lives on the page's root hosting view, so the old `holdScrolling` ancestor walk never found the list's scroll view.
- **Fix:** never simultaneous with recognizers on views above the chart; from the moment a second finger lands (or a knob drag starts) every recognizer on those views is switched off and exactly those are switched back on when the fingers lift; the scroll view is found from the touch's own view. One-finger drags on the chart's empty space still scroll the list and move the sheet as anywhere else (a decision — reversible).
- **Test:** `testSoundChartZoom` now pinches in (rubber band), turns two fingers, pinches out, pinches back past 1×, pinned and unpinned, asserting the sheet stays open, its frame doesn't move or shrink, the list doesn't scroll, and an in-app probe (`sound.pageShift`, demo mode only) reads 0 page movement. **Passes (193 s); with the isolation reverted it fails** at "Pinch in at full width: the Speed and Audio sheet stays open".

## 3. Core AI (area 2)

### 3.1 What the engine source and his phone show

- **Source (coreai-models, `CoreAIPipelinedEngine.swift`):** `_encodeStepForConstrainedGeneration` (used by `generateConstrained` ← `PipelinedConstrainedDecodingStrategy`) binds no static inputs (Gemma 4's `ple_table`/`ple_scale`), no extra states (hybrids' `convState`), no per-token inputs; `_encodeNextStepGPU` and `_encodeChunk` bind all of them. Its jump-forward feeds `[lastToken] + jumpTokens` in one step — several tokens into a decode-only (S=1) graph. **His request's diagnosis of the constrained path is correct.**
- **Phone, Gemma 4 E2B (two closures on e929aad, 11:29/11:30Z, "Reading part 1 of 3 · 774 input tokens"):** MetricKit crash (diagnostic-1791977368/-977400): `EXC_BREAKPOINT`/SIGTRAP, a Swift trap inside `CoreAIRuntime`. Symbolicated against an exact e929aad Release rebuild: the PodSkipper caller is `EngineImpl.processChunkedInput` (resume point of the S=1 `_encodeChunk` loop, line 1959) — **prefill, before any constrained step**, through the path that *does* bind the tables. His run said "**compiled on this iPhone when first used** · word tables in memory": the h17p copy wasn't installed. CoreAIKit (`KitGemmaModel.swift`, `ModelCatalog.swift`): "iOS MUST use the AOT-compiled bundle — the plain bundle's ~2 GB of graph constants crash the on-device GPU specializer (`LLVM ERROR: Failed to allocate mmap'd buffer`)". So the phone closures were the on-device specializer; the unbound-tables constrained step would have been the next failure (and is the likely cause of the 14 Oct Mac hang, which ran that path).
- **Downloaded Gemma tokenizer:** at revision d669ab2 it *does* carry a chat template (`{{ bos_token }}<|turn>role\n…<turn|>\n…<|turn>model\n`), written with the download — the request's "no embedded template" doesn't hold for this revision. Rendering explicitly anyway: strings and token ids identical (checked with the bundle's tokenizer; `<bos>` = 2).
- **Neural Engine / background:** `coreai-build inspect` and the files: his h17p compiled copies — Qwen3 4B (compiled with `--preferred-compute neural-engine` in Pass 35) and Gemma 4 E2B — hold **only an `MPSGraph` delegate** (2.1 GB GPU executable), no Neural Engine region. Pass 35's note "ANE regions for h17p present" is not borne out by the files. No Core AI language-model build available to his phone avoids the GPU, so none can run backgrounded without Background GPU Access.
- **The 1,024 cap:** CoreAIKit (TypedDecisions notes): the cap is the *pipelined* engine's growing KV cache on iOS (#124); the *sequential* engine read prompts up to 3,789 tokens correctly on an iPhone 17 Pro (MiniCPM5 2B).

### 3.3 TypedDecisions — evaluated, not adopted this pass

One forward pass scores a multiple-choice question at the answer slot (sequential engine, logits). For PodSkipper it would classify a candidate span the reader already bounded ("paid ad / host read / plug / other show / the show") with no format failures. Costs: a separate decision bundle (`qwen3.5-2b-decision`, `decider-0.8b`) download; prefill cost per span similar to now (decode ports read one token per step); it doesn't find boundaries. Worth a Mac-lab trial on the fixtures' reader spans before any app change; not built.

## 4. Prompt plans (area 3)

`ModelPromptPlan` now decides per family and size for both runtimes (identity includes the runtime): Qwen3/3.5 (≤1B → 1,800-token stretches), Gemma 3/4 (reasoned answer; Gemma 4 turns), LFM2.5 (Liquid sampling, thinking block closed), Granite H (repetition penalty, prose → list), MiniCPM5, Phi (fences; Phi-3-mini-4k 2,500), Ministral, Llama 3.2/Dolphin and Bonsai/Ternary Bonsai (a cut over 60 % of a ≥4-min stretch dropped), SmolLM3 (penalty, loop stop). Every free-text answer (MLX retry, Core AI pipelined) starts as `{"parts":[` and stops when the list closes. Core AI uses the same plan; ~1,000-token models get the **candidate** profile: rules measured at 125 (Qwen3), 140 (Gemma 4), 147 (LFM2.5) tokens, a 3-part answer, ~690 transcript tokens a part. Label aliases (AD, SPONSOR, SELF_PROMOTION, CROSS_PROMO, CONVERSATION…); "PROMO" alone is not guessed. Real-episode quality per family: **unmeasured** (phone or fixture lab).
