## 2. What differed from Apple, and what changed

| Destination | Apple (reference) | PodSkipper before | Now |
|---|---|---|---|
| Search tab | Guide screenshot: large "Search" on the bar's line, a full-width field under it reading the source ("Apple Podcasts"), categories below | No field: a minimized magnifier at the top right (`searchToolbarBehavior(.minimize)`, 97d39cd); the tab opened on categories with nowhere to type | Field always under the title (`navigationBarDrawer(.always)`), placeholder "Apple Podcasts" / "Library" by scope (27.2 `SEARCH_SOURCE_STORE`, `SEARCH_LIBRARY_PLACEHOLDER`); scopes still appear while searching. A pinned field collapsed a plain large title to a small one and the automatic drawer hid the field until a pull — `toolbarTitleDisplayMode(.inlineLarge)` gives both, as Apple's screenshot shows |
| Tab roots (Library, Up Next, New, Settings) | The same inline large title on the bar's line | A large title on its own line under the toolbar | `.inlineLarge` on every tab root: one look, content starts higher |
| Library toolbar | ⋯ More holds New Station (guide), Edit Library, Follow a Show by URL… (27.2 strings) | A filter-icon menu and a bare "+" in one capsule | One ⋯ "Library Options": Edit Library, New Station (made and opened in its editor, as the Stations page does), Follow a Show…, then sort and view options; search stays its own button (Pass 39's reason: nothing slides in above the pinned activity bar). The add sheet is titled "Follow a Show" (it takes a name or an RSS address, so "by URL" would undersell it) |
| Settings | — (PodSkipper's own page) | ~58 pt of empty space under the totals: a 12-pt spacer row stood a list row's minimum height | The space is the totals row's own padding |

Checked and left as they are:
- **Episode menu wording** already Apple's (Mark as Played, Go to Show, Share Episode…, Copy Link, Save Episode).
- **Library row order** is user-editable since X35-18 (Edit Library), so the default isn't reordered under him.
- **Now Playing** follows Apple's iOS 27 order since Pass 46.
- **Transcript view:** Apple's runtime transcript layout can't be inspected (no Podcasts app in the simulator, no guide screenshot of it), so it's left as is rather than guessed.

Observed once, not reproduced: in one tour run the Library listed one of the demo's three shows after a search; three later runs with the same steps listed all three (`build/shots-p48tour3/named/p48-library-*.png`).
