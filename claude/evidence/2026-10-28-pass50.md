## 2. What changed

| Before | Now |
|---|---|
| One row per sentence | `TranscriptLayout`: timed paragraphs (a new one after a pause at a sentence's end, or after 4 sentences / 25 s; never past 8 lines), each under its time; chapter titles between paragraphs; "· · ·" (read as "Silence") for gaps of 8 s or more |
| The current sentence bold, the rest dim | The paragraph being said lights word by word — from each word's own time when the transcript kept them, else in proportion — with the rest of it dimmer; only that paragraph's view reads the playhead (`ActiveParagraph`) |
| A search field always at the top | Search appears when you scroll the transcript (with **Back to Now**); it has **Match Case** and **Whole Words** options and **Done**; following resumes six seconds after you stop scrolling |
| Tap a line to play | Tap a paragraph to play from it; touch and hold: **Play from m:ss**, **Share from m:ss…** (the player's Apple-link share), **Copy** |
| — | Footer: "Transcript created on this iPhone" (every PodSkipper transcript is made on the device) |

The first simulator photograph had an eleven-row paragraph in the ~340-point window, which would push the words being said out of view, so paragraphs were shortened before this pass's final run.
