## 1. Core AI Qwen3 4B closing PodSkipper (export + source)

**22 closures on 19 Oct (05:39–08:55 UTC), every one while Core AI Qwen3 4B read an episode**, at a prompt of 2,045–3,959 tokens (most of them on Leroy Jetson #960, parts 1 and 2 of 5 at 2,752 and 3,959 tokens, as he pressed Find Ads Again), with the stage string "CoreAIPipelinedEngine · compiled on this iPhone when first used · Core AI's own answer format". Several were mid-answer ("writing classification · 144 tokens"; "part 5 of 5 … 1 tokens"). MetricKit holds no crash report for them — what iOS leaves when it ends an app for memory. Core AI MiniCPM5 2B read the same 13 episodes on the sequential engine (held to 2,047 tokens) with no closure.

Source (coreai-models, as vendored): PodSkipper sent Qwen3's unhinted GPU builds to the pipelined engine's own grammar decoder (`.runtimeGrammar`, `runConstrainedCompletion`). That path grows a dynamic KV state to the whole prompt and answer and has none of the iOS guard plain `generate()` carries ("miscompiles this graph class once the bound KV state's seq dim reaches 2048"). PodSkipper let it hold 4,096 tokens. Qwen3 4B's KV is ~147 KB a token (36 layers × 8 KV heads × 128 × K and V × fp16): ~600 MB at 4,096 on top of ~2.5 GB of weights.

**Strongest supported reading:** the closures come from reading 2,000–4,000-token parts on that path (memory, and/or the ≥ 2,048-position problem). Not proven: no crash report, and no phone here.

The launch note blamed "most likely compiling the model on the phone" for every closure, including ones mid-answer on part 5 of 5. Wrong; replaced (below).

## 6. Changes

Commits: 9fbd4f8 (A) and f639f16 (B), on top of f34df0c; unsigned IPA run 39313279595 on f639f16 — success (release `latest`, PodSkipper.ipa 72.8 MB, 19 Oct 19:59 UTC).

- **Playback.** `AudioEngine` reopens the file after a failed read (0.2, 1, 3, 6 s) and reads on from the same frame; a reopen in progress isn't "starved" (10 s grace); a file that stays unreadable is `.unreadable` → paused at the heard position with "Paused: this episode's audio couldn't be read. Play to try again." (Play reopens it). Error words carry the four-character code. `routeDisconnected` doesn't hold the sound check off; a successful play clears the flag. After three restarts in two minutes: "Paused: the sound kept stopping while the iPhone was busy. Play to carry on."
- **Core AI.** Unhinted dynamic bundles (Qwen3's GPU builds) read on the sequential engine (2,047 on iOS), as MiniCPM5 does; the grammar decoder is only their fallback, at 1,024. Consequence: Qwen3 4B now plans 2,047 tokens, so it gets the compact rules and shorter parts (`Profile.forContext`) — quality and speed that way not measured. `CoreAIMemory`: an iOS memory warning since the model loaded, or under 300 MB left, stops the read between parts (owed; counts as a try). Launch note says what happened: stage, free memory at the last part, compile only while loading, background closures named as such.
- **Resume.** The in-flight note records being off screen and free memory. A closure off screen isn't counted against the model. Closures counted per episode: one → one more try of its own; two → the episode waits for him (`Run.waitsForYou`), still owed. Closures before Pass 43 (`CoreAIStability.policy`) don't block automatic work. A read that couldn't finish keeps an earlier model's result *for now* but stays owed (`modelPending` + `ModelFinder.owe`), and is read again when PodSkipper is open (catch-up tries bound it). A job told to wait (errors while away) no longer holds up the line (`headOfLine`).
- **Reader.** Agreement labels only (two families, or the ad-free proof); promo share 0.395; model reads together ≤ 0.5× the anchor; one in five agreement episodes held out and checked; training only on new data, and after a rollback only with a new correction or 20 % more; one-model labels dropped once (`silverPolicy`); the learned head in use stays.
- **Prompt.** `Profile.listener` = v2r for Qwen3.5 > 2.5B.
- **Library.** Find Ads Again for selections (bar: "Find Again", read out "Find Ads Again"; ⋯ item when the selection mixes done and not done) on every selection bar, keeping reviewed cuts; Select first in the episode long press and every ⋯ menu; Up Next: Select, the shared selection bar, "Find Ads in All"; Activity rows name engine and model and say kept / reads it later / waits for you (`ProcessingPipeline.provenance`); the Now card names the model reading.
