## 9. Reader: external data, boundaries and architecture, on held-out shows (batch C)

**Where the Reader stands.** Against each model's own cuts on the same labelled episodes (the Reader as the phone ran it, with its audio evidence): the Reader hears 27–33 s/h, the models alone 62 (MLX Qwen3.5 4B) to 234 (Bonsai 2) — it already misses far less than any model; it cuts more of the show than MLX Qwen3.5 4B alone (≈ 18 vs 8.3 s/h). Its break-level edges are tight (median 1–2 s; the per-read ±30 s in `phone_eval` is adjacent reads joined into one cut). Speed: 7–20 s per episode on the phone against 100–630 s for the models. So the work is show cut, outros and own plugs, not speed.

**Protocol.** `reader_distill.py showfolds` (new): three folds that each hold out *whole shows* of the 71 labelled episodes (25/23/23 episodes; no episode of a held-out show, labelled or not, and no lab fixture of it is trained on), one reader per fold (seed 7), the app's detector (`lab-segments`, transcript only — no audio evidence, so intros/outros the phone finds by repeated audio aren't credited here). Scores: `phone_eval` summary, `perclass.py` (heard and mostly-missed per class), `sentence_eval.py` (held-out sentence probabilities before the detector).

**External data (new, `Tools/ReaderData/README.md`):** 34 YouTube podcast episodes from 18 channels in other genres (interview, science, business, news, sports, true crime, comedy, pop culture; none of his shows), labelled by SponsorBlock users (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), audio transcribed here by the app's own `TranscriptionService`: 37,348 sentences (856 ad, 117 own-plug, 21 intro, 30 outro, 36 k show). Stored in `~/Developer/pk-data/sponsorblock/` (not in the repo).

- **What improved:** the readers' ranking of promotional sentences on unseen shows — every variant with boundary weighting or external data has higher average precision for ads, and the external data more than triples intro AP (0.079 → 0.274; 0.359 with both). Fewer ads heard (−1.7 s/h) and fewer missed breaks.
- **What didn't:** each buys its recall with precision, and the detector turns that into more show cut (+0.8 to +6.5 s/h). The detector's calibration knob for the Reader (`tagBoost`) moves results ≤ 1 s/h across 0.6–1.0 (sweep: folds 1–2 choose, fold 0 checks), so it isn't a calibration fix. The external data's extra show cut is concentrated in the two public-radio shows (99% Invisible 62 → 118, This American Life 23 → 49 s/h), while it nearly removed Peter Attia's (54 → 1.5) — genre coverage of the external set decides where it helps.
- **The context layer** (a convolution over neighbouring sentences' head outputs, implemented in Python and briefly in Swift) didn't help; the Swift side was removed.
- **Decision: no Reader shipped.** None beats the shipped recipe on both heard and show cut; his targets weigh show cut twice. Shipped readers unchanged. Single-seed folds: differences of ±2 s/h are within run-to-run variation.
- **Where the heard time is:** Your Mom's House (186 s/h, every variant — closing songs of 60–326 s) and Legion of Skanks (59 s/h — long plug stretches) are about half of all heard time; outros are ~39 % of it. A transcript reader can't hear a song.

## 10. Apple's sound classifier for intros and outros (lab lead, not integrated)

`Tools/DetectionLab/LabMusic.swift` (SoundAnalysis `SNClassifySoundRequest(.version1)`, 3-s windows, 1.5-s hop; ~25 s per 90-min episode on this Mac): on the 16 lab fixtures (exact audio of their labels, 49 k windows) its "music" confidence separates intro/outro windows from the rest with **AUC 0.95** (ads 0.57). A rule — sustained music touching the first/last 3 minutes, ≥ 15 s — covers 60 % of the fixtures' 418 s of intro/outro for 52 s of show cut over ~24 h (34 % for 7 s when speech-like windows are excluded). On a fresh download of YMH "Prozac Princess" it finds about 190 s of the 326-s closing song the Reader misses. **Not integrated:** his labelled episodes' audio isn't on the Mac with the phone's timeline (ads stitched in at download move it), so the effect on his episodes can't be measured yet.
