## 4. Drag to choose (Pass 41 B)

`SelectionDrag` (Views/CollectionKit.swift): touch a row's tick and slide up or down through the ticks next to it. Starting on an unticked row ticks the run; on a ticked one, unticks it. Each move recomputes the run from the selection as it was before the drag, so rows crossed again go back to what they were (no flicker). Holding within 36 pt of the list's visible top or bottom for 0.35 s scrolls it a row a tenth of a second and carries the run on. The tick column has its own tap (a quick tap there had opened the episode through the list row). While a run is being chosen the list holds still (`scrollDisabled`); a drag anywhere else on a row scrolls as before. Cancelled touches reset.

Simulator findings on the way (each fixed): the list's pan took over two rows in; a drag's location inside list cells came back in the tick's own coordinates, so everything is in screen coordinates; the last visible row sat in the edge band (now a 0.35 s hold first). The list's layout frame is the part no bar covers on both collections and the show page (logged: 122–689 and 122–733 pt; the scroll view runs on under the bars, inset), so that frame is the edge; subtracting the safe area or scroll insets as well double-counted them (tried, reverted).

Pages: Library collections (Downloaded, Saved, Latest, Recently Played), a show's episodes (browsing and publish selection), Stations (grouped by show too), Bookmarks, Activity › Finished, Ad-Free Feeds (Publish). Up Next has no multi-select (only Reorder); Settings' storage/log lists keep their own taps.
