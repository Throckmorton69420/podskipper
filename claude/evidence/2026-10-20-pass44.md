## 3. The Reader, retrained as a whole (lab)

Pass 43 showed the Reader's small head can't learn its way out: every head-only variant moved along the same trade-off. This pass retrains **the whole reader** — the 14 M-parameter ELECTRA-small body that reads each sentence with the minute around it, plus its head — from the pretrained ELECTRA-small, on (`Tools/DetectionLab/reader_distill.py`):

- the 17 lab fixtures (as the shipped readers),
- the 58 labelled phone episodes (EITHER sentences left out),
- his reviewed cuts, overriding any other label, at twice the weight,
- (episodes nobody labelled would come in only where two model families agree, Pass 43's 98 %-precise rule; with 58 of 60 labelled, none remained). Nothing a single engine said is used as a label.

The shipped readers were trained on the 17 fixtures plus his phone episodes labelled by *one* engine's cuts at 0.4 weight; that is the main thing replaced.

**Held-out test.** Three folds over the 58 episodes; each fold's two readers (seeds 7 and 11, averaged as the app averages its two) never saw that fold's episodes or any copy of them. Their sentence answers go through **the app's own detector** (`lab-segments`, `SegmentDetector` with the probabilities handed in), and the cuts are scored against the labels next to the shipped readers on the same episodes. "Break edges" is new: where the skip a listener hears starts and ends against the labelled break (back-to-back spots and the EITHER between them count as one break), median seconds.

| Reader, every episode held out | Heard s/h | Show cut s/h | Breaks missed | Wrong kind | Break start / end ± s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shipped | 37.6 | 22.4 | 69/409 | 41 | 2.6 / 1.1 |
| **Retrained (Pass 44)** | **27.4** | **19.2** | **37/409** | **37** | **2.0 / 0.9** |

Missed, by kind (shipped → retrained): ads 6 → 6 of 237, own and guest plugs 21 → 12 of 91, network promos 4 → 0 of 18, intros 13 → 5 of 16, outros 25 → 14 of 47. The earlier "± 30 s" edge figures were per spot: a break of three spots cut as one skip counted two wrong edges each.

## 4. The four models, each with its own instructions (lab)

**Method.** The MLX lab (`Tools/MLXLab`, the app's own prompt code linked in) reads, for each model, the same stretches the app would read (`phone_judge.py`, the app's focus ranges around the shipped Reader's cuts) on 25 labelled episodes kept out of the variants' design (`Tools/DetectionLab/model-dev.json`), with grammar-held JSON answers as on his phone. The Core AI models are read by their MLX versions held to Core AI's room on his phone (2,047 tokens: 1,450 of transcript, 170 of answer). Scored by `model_lab_score.py` against the labels; these are **the models' own parts, before the app's cut check**. "Break edges" are 0 for the models because they answer by line and usually name the labelled line exactly.

Variants: **listener** (shipped since Pass 43 for Qwen3.5 > 2.5 B); **v3 = `Profile.listenerEdges`** (the listener rules + how an episode opens and closes, and a round of plugs as one entry per person — where §2 showed every model losing time); **compact** (shipped for Core AI at 2,047); **cplus = `Profile.compactListener`** (the listener's labels and key rules in ~230 tokens, four-part answer, the task said again after the stretch).

**Decisions** (his targets weigh show cut twice as heavily as ads heard):

## 5. Library: one episode row, Activity during Find Ads (source + unit + sim)

**What differed (source).** Five row designs showed an episode: the show page and Up Next drew `EpisodeRow` (date line, 22 pt title, notes, 90 pt cover, Play pill, Find Ads, ⋯); Latest Episodes, Downloaded, Saved, Recently Played, Stations, Library search, people pages and the episode page's other episodes drew `EpisodeCompactRow` (cover on the left, 17 pt title, a round play button, no date line or notes, no ⋯); choosing several on a show page swapped in yet another row; Activity drew its own 48 pt rows with no controls. Swipes differed on every page (Play Next / Save; Archive / Played; Top / Remove / Played; Remove; Forget; none), and so did how choosing several looked.

**Now (source).** One `EpisodeListRow` (`Views/EpisodeListRow.swift`) on every list: the full row, the same touch-and-hold and ⋯ menu, the same ticks and drag-to-choose, and Apple Podcasts' own swipes (27.2 b2 strings: "Mark as Played" to the right; to the left the page's own removal, then "Save"/"Unsave"). A page adds only what is its own: a status line under the notes (Up Next: "getting ready"; Activity: "Next in line · about 4 min", why a job paused, why it failed, and for finished episodes "3 cuts · 2 min 45 s · MLX · Qwen3.5 4B · 2 hours ago"), the words on Find Ads (Activity: "Resume", "Try Again"), and its removal ("Delete" for a download, "Unsave", "Remove" from Up Next or the line, "Forget" a paused job, "Archive" on a show). The compact row, the selecting row and Activity's rows are deleted. The episode page's other episodes are titled "More Episodes", as Apple's.

**Activity during Find Ads (source + unit).** The job store (`ProcessingJobStore`) is observed by the line, the paused and failed lists, the activity bar and every row's Find Ads button. Each progress step of a running job changed its record, so all of those redrew several times a second — and each redraw re-sorted every job record (once per row: "is this one waiting?"). Now progress within a step changes no observed state (`revision` moves on every other change), the sorted order is computed once per change, and `storageError` is assigned only when it changes. `Pass44Tests.testProgressWithinAStepDoesNotRedrawTheLine`: 19 progress updates, no redraw; a new step, a reorder do. Activity's finished rows no longer read each episode's cuts and search the attempt log on every redraw (worked out once per reload, cuts fetched with the episodes in one read), and the Now card's lines are held to two lines each so the list doesn't shift under the finger each second. Not measured on a phone; the simulator scroll metric (`ResponsivenessLabUITests.testActivityScrollHitches`) is in §7.

**Screens (sim, demo library):** Latest Episodes, Downloaded, Saved (and choosing), Activity scrolled (line, paused, finished) — `build/shots-p44lib/named/`.

**Apple Podcasts parity (source).** On top of the rows and swipes above: the play pill says how much is left once an episode is started ("23m left", Apple's "%@ left"), the total before that; the ⋯ button is "More" to VoiceOver (Apple's `AX_CONTEXT_MENU_BUTTON_LABEL`); the episode page's other episodes are "More Episodes". The rest of the destination audit (player, Search, New, show header, settings) is not done this pass. Apple 27.2 b2 calls the list you build "Queue" ("Add to Queue", "Clear Queue", "Your Queue is Empty") while "Up Next" is its Home shelf; PodSkipper keeps "Up Next" for his list — renaming it is his call, not made here.
