## 2. Symbolication (exact commit 4fdd2b7 rebuilt Release iphoneos; iOS 27.0.1 device symbols)

- **06:45 termination:** main thread `PodSkipperApp.body` scene-phase closure → `CloudSync.push()` → `-[NSUserDefaults setObject:forKey:]` → `xpc_connection_send_message_with_reply_sync` (waiting on the settings service) while iOS was ending the app. The phone was hot and Core AI preparation had just written gigabytes. **Fix:** no `synchronize()` in push (iCloud sends on its own); records-of-what-happened writes (`cloud.lastSent`, `cloud.lastReceived*`, the sync ledger) go through `PreferenceWriter` on a background queue; the ledger keeps its last-saved value in memory so a queued write can't be read stale.
- **01:30 termination:** main thread `EpisodeNotesText.body` task → `EpisodeNotesText.parse` → `NSAttributedString(data:options:[.documentType: .html])` (UIFoundation) — the HTML import drives a hidden web view and waits for it on the main thread; in the background it exceeded iOS's 30 s. **Fix:** `HTMLText.linked` — a WebKit-free parser (same tag handling as `strip`, keeps `http/https/mailto/tel` links), run detached off the main thread.

## 3. Gemma 4 E2B — what was wrong and what is possible

Pass 36 marked it unsupported. Re-examined against CoreAIKit 0.7.3 source, its catalog and the model repo (`mlboydaisuke/gemma-4-E2B-CoreAI` @ d669ab2b):

- The 9.6 TB `ple_table` error was PodSkipper's: it loaded the decoder through the generic runner, which leaves the decoder's two static table inputs unbound. CoreAIKit loads it with `EngineFactory.createEngine(…, options: EngineOptions(staticInputBuffers:))`, binding `ple_table` ← `embed_per_layer.i8` and `ple_scale` ← `embed_per_layer.scale.f32` from `ios-frontend/gemma4_qat_gather_raw` (QAT decoder needs QAT tables). PodSkipper now does the same (`CoreAIGemmaTables`, owned buffers when memory allows, else copy-on-write mapping), and downloads just those two files (2.35 GB) into the decoder's folder (`ple-tables/`).
- iPhone build: the publisher's iOS decoder is AOT-compiled for **h18p only**; their card says the plain bundle crashed the on-device GPU specializer on early iOS 27 betas (not re-tested on the release OS). His phone reports increased-memory **Present**, 6.38 GB available — the measured 4.4 GB peak fits.
- **Mac, measured:** `coreai-build compile <b2 QAT _tbl decoder> --platform iOS --preferred-compute gpu --architecture h17p --expect-frequent-reshapes` → 18.6 s, 176 MB peak footprint, output 2.0 GB `gemma4_e2b_qat_decode_int4lin_tbl.h17p.aimodelc` with `sourceHash FC6830D0…` = the download's `main.hash`; same layout as the publisher's h18p copy (`main-h17p-delegates/MPSGraph/…resources.bin` 2.09 GB). At `build/coreai-aot/gemma/` (git-ignored).
- **Mac, measured:** the lab (`Tools/CoreAILab`, `LAB_TABLES=`) loaded the decoder with tables bound in 35 s (context 4,096; iPhone cap 1,024). While reading the first part the Mac hit a **hardware watchdog reset** (`ResetCounter … Boot faults: wdog,reset_in1`, 03:40). Not repeated. So: binding verified on the Mac, Gemma quality on his shows **not measured**, and the Mac's GPU hang is a warning sign, not phone evidence (CoreAIKit reports 30 tok/s on iPhone 17 Pro through this exact path).
- The raw-Metal Gemma runtime in CoreAIKit (`gemma-4-e2b-metal`, chip-independent) would avoid AOT entirely, but its weight pack is unpublished ("not published yet; re-add when it ships"). Recorded as the next option.
- Gemma 4 E4B: no iPhone build in the catalog (static-table form exceeds the iPhone memory budget per CoreAIKit); unchanged. Gemma 4 E2B / E4B and Gemma 3 4B already run through MLX on his phone.

## 4. Chart zoom (Gemini's spec §2)

Domain-space zoom 1–4× (rubber band to 0.92, snaps to 1× below 1.05 on release), pan clamped to `0…1−1/zoom`, `screenX = (normX − pan)·zoom·W` for curves (wider drawing, not `scaleEffect`), zones, knobs and zone names; dB scale and Louder/Quieter fixed. One UIKit recognizer for two-finger pinch + pan (centroid-anchored), which also sees every finger: a second finger within 75 ms of the first restores a knob's strength, later stops the drag where it is; the enclosing list stops scrolling while it runs. Octave ruler (EQ bands) fades in from 1.25×, half-octave marks from 2.2× or while a knob is dragged; drag callout "<fix> · <Hz> · <dB>"; 3 pt viewport track; glass "N.N× ↺" pill; double tap resets / zooms 2.2× at the tap; a tap on a knob with another within 34 pt at 1× zooms 2.5× on it; VoiceOver zoom action. Knobs stay vertical-only: each fix acts at a fixed frequency in the audio engine, so sideways dragging would change what the controls mean (Gemini's closing question — declined). Its optional "auto-focus on slider" idea: the chart already highlights the curve of the fix being changed for 4 s; zooming the pinned chart automatically was not added (it needs a new setting and would move the chart under his finger).
